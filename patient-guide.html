Prepare for a more informed appointment

A simple guide to preparing questions, sharing relevant information and understanding follow-up. Your treating clinician’s instructions take priority.

Before your appointment

  • Write down when symptoms started, how often they occur and what makes them better or worse.
  • Bring a list of medicines, supplements, allergies and relevant medical conditions.
  • Bring previous test reports or treatment records if requested.
  • Ask the hospital whether you need to fast, change medication or make other preparations. Do not change medication unless your clinician advises it.
  • Ask about consultation fees, appointment timing, accessibility and whether a support person may accompany you.

Questions to ask your clinician

  • What are the possible causes of my symptoms?
  • What examination or tests do you recommend, and why?
  • What treatment options are suitable for my case?
  • What are the benefits, risks, alternatives and costs?
  • What recovery time and follow-up should I plan for?
  • Which symptoms should prompt an urgent call or emergency visit?

After a procedure or treatment

Follow the specific discharge instructions provided by your treating team. Confirm how to take prescribed medicines, care for the treatment area, manage bowel movements, resume activities and arrange follow-up. If instructions are unclear, contact the care team rather than relying on general online advice.

When to seek urgent medical attention

Seek prompt medical evaluation for significant or persistent rectal bleeding, black or tarry stools, severe or worsening pain, fever with a painful swelling, pus with systemic symptoms, fainting or symptoms that rapidly deteriorate. For a medical emergency, use local emergency services.

Privacy when contacting the hospital

Share only the information needed to arrange care. Avoid posting medical reports, identity documents or detailed health information in public comments or unsecured channels. Ask the hospital how it handles patient information and appointment records.

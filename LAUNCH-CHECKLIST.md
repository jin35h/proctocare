# ProctoCare launch checklist

## Clinical accuracy
- [ ] Verify hospital contact details, hours, services and facilities.
- [ ] Verify doctor qualifications, registration and scope of practice.
- [ ] Confirm all treatment, technology, insurance and outcome claims.
- [ ] Add genuine author/reviewer names, credentials, actual dates and sources.
- [ ] Do not fabricate testimonials, ratings, patient numbers or outcomes.

## SEO / local discovery
- [ ] Confirm canonical host and HTTPS redirects.
- [ ] Validate metadata, structured data and sitemap.
- [ ] Submit sitemap to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- [ ] Keep NAP consistent with verified Google Business Profile and directories.
- [ ] Add approved favicon and social sharing image.
- [ ] Avoid thin doorway pages for nearby towns.

## Privacy / accessibility / QA
- [ ] Finalise privacy, terms and consent wording to match real data flows.
- [ ] Verify WhatsApp form destination and consent.
- [ ] Test keyboard focus, contrast, labels, screen-reader names and reduced motion.
- [ ] Check all links and mobile breakpoints.
- [ ] Compress images and test Core Web Vitals.
- [ ] Validate HTML/schema and remove all placeholders before launch.

How to use references responsibly

Check the current version and applicability to the Indian clinical context. Do not copy large passages. Summarise in original language, cite the evidence for specific claims and have a qualified clinician review the final page. Remove or replace any link that becomes outdated or no longer supports the claim.

Clinical page citation checklist

  • Source title, publisher and direct URL.
  • Publication or last-updated date, when available.
  • Date accessed for sources that may change.
  • Exact claims supported by each source.
  • Clinician reviewer and last review date.

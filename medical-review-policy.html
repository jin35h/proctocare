Medical Review Policy

A transparent framework for clinical review of ProctoCare educational content.

What should be reviewed

Pages discussing symptoms, causes, diagnosis, investigations, medication, procedures, complications, recovery or prevention should be reviewed by an appropriately qualified clinician before publication and periodically thereafter.

Reviewer qualifications

The hospital should verify relevant degrees, professional registration and scope of practice. The reviewer’s profile should accurately describe the role they performed. Do not label content “medically reviewed” when no substantive review took place.

Review process

  1. Identify each clinical claim and its source.
  2. Check that the wording reflects current evidence and communicates uncertainty fairly.
  3. Confirm safety warnings, contraindications and advice to seek care.
  4. Check that treatment choices are presented without guarantees or unjustified superiority claims.
  5. Record reviewer, date, version and required changes.

Review frequency

Set a risk-based review schedule and revisit pages sooner when guidelines change, a safety issue emerges, or the hospital changes its services. Do not invent a review date; record the date the review actually occurred.

Corrections and accountability

Provide a process for reporting errors, triaging safety-critical issues and correcting the page. Assign a named editorial owner and clinician responsible for approving clinical content.

Current site status

This is a recommended policy framework. Before launch, ProctoCare should identify the actual clinical reviewer and editorial owner, document the workflow and apply it to each health page.

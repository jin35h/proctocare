Editorial Policy

How ProctoCare health content should be created, checked, referenced and updated.

Purpose and audience

Health content should help patients and caregivers understand medical terminology, prepare questions and know when to seek professional care. It must not pressure readers into a procedure or promise a particular result.

Authorship and clinical review

Every clinical article should identify its author and, where applicable, a qualified medical reviewer with verified credentials. Do not display a medical reviewer’s name without their consent and actual review.

Evidence and references

Use current clinical guidelines, peer-reviewed research, recognised public-health organisations and authoritative medical institutions. Prefer original or primary sources when appropriate. Distinguish established evidence from uncertainty and avoid unsupported superiority claims.

Updates and corrections

Display “last medically reviewed” and “last updated” dates only when those actions occurred. Keep a review log, correct material errors promptly and update pages when clinical guidance or service details change.

AI-assisted drafting

AI tools may assist with outlining or plain-language editing, but they do not replace expert judgement, source verification or clinician review. Editors must verify every medical claim, citation, name, statistic and treatment detail before publication.

Commercial transparency

Clearly disclose relevant commercial relationships, sponsorships or conflicts of interest. Educational content should not disguise advertising as independent clinical advice.

Patient stories, images and testimonials

Use identifiable patient material only with valid, documented permission and a clear explanation of how it will be used. Do not fabricate testimonials, alter clinical evidence or imply that an individual outcome is typical.

Page-level disclosure

Each clinical page should show author, reviewer, credentials, published date, review date, sources and a concise medical disclaimer. Replace this policy’s draft status with a named responsible editor and approved contact before launch.

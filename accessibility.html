Accessibility Statement

Accessibility goals for the ProctoCare Hospital website.

Our accessibility goal

The website is designed to support keyboard navigation, readable contrast, responsive layouts, descriptive image alternatives, visible focus states and reduced-motion preferences. Accessibility is an ongoing process, not a one-time certification.

Known limitations

This starter template has not undergone a formal accessibility audit. Third-party images, embedded services and future changes may introduce barriers. Do not claim WCAG conformance until the site has been tested against the relevant standard.

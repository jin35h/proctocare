Medical Disclaimer

Important information about the limits of online medical content.

Educational information only

Content on this website is intended for general educational and informational purposes. It is not a substitute for a consultation, diagnosis, treatment plan or advice from a qualified healthcare professional who knows your individual circumstances.

No diagnosis or treatment relationship

Reading this website, sending an enquiry or using its general information does not by itself establish a doctor-patient relationship. Do not use website content to diagnose yourself, delay care, or start, stop or change medication.

Individual outcomes vary

Medical conditions, treatment suitability, risks, recovery time and outcomes vary by patient. No outcome, cure, pain level, recovery duration or absence of recurrence is guaranteed. Discuss your individual case with the treating clinician.

Emergency and urgent symptoms

This website is not an emergency service and may not be monitored continuously. For severe pain, heavy bleeding, fainting, fever with a painful swelling or rapidly worsening symptoms, seek urgent medical evaluation. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.

External links and references

Links to third-party websites are provided for convenience or further reading. ProctoCare should review external resources before linking and cannot guarantee that third-party information remains current or accurate.

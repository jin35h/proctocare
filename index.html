A considered care journey

Know what to expect before you visit

Discuss your symptoms privately, understand the purpose of an examination or test, and ask about benefits, risks, alternatives, costs and recovery before making a treatment decision.

  • Share symptoms and relevant medical history with the clinician.
  • Ask which tests, if any, are appropriate and why.
  • Discuss non-surgical and surgical options when clinically relevant.
  • Request written instructions about medicines, preparation and recovery.

Common questions

Helpful answers before you contact us

What conditions does ProctoCare focus on?

The hospital website describes care for piles (haemorrhoids), anal fissure, anal fistula, pilonidal sinus, constipation and other anorectal concerns. A clinician must assess symptoms to determine the cause and appropriate care.

Does every patient need surgery?

No. Treatment depends on the diagnosis, severity, medical history and clinical assessment. Some conditions can be managed without surgery; procedures are considered when appropriate.

Can I ask about laser treatment?

Yes. Ask the clinician whether a laser-based procedure is appropriate for your specific diagnosis, and discuss evidence, risks, alternatives, cost and recovery. A laser procedure is not automatically the best option for every patient.

How do I book an appointment?

Call +91 94281 35435 or use the appointment enquiry form. An enquiry is not a confirmed appointment until the hospital responds.

Is website information a substitute for a consultation?

No. Website content is educational and cannot diagnose a condition or replace personalised medical advice. Seek prompt medical care for severe or worsening symptoms.

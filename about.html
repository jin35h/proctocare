Our approach

Clear information. Respectful conversations.

Health concerns involving bowel movements or the anal and rectal area can feel difficult to discuss. Patient-centred care begins with respectful communication, appropriate clinical assessment and clear explanations of available options.

  • Protect patient dignity and confidentiality.
  • Explain investigations and treatment choices in plain language.
  • Discuss risks, benefits, alternatives and costs before decisions.
  • Provide understandable preparation and follow-up instructions.
